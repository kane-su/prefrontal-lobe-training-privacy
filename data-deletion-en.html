Data Deletion

How to delete your data (without deleting your account) · Effective 2026-09-11

This page applies to the "Prefrontal Trainer" (前额叶训练) app. You can delete local or cloud training data without deleting your account by following the steps below.

1Delete local data (in-app, no account deletion)

  1. Open "Prefrontal Trainer" → the "Stats" tab, open a session's details, and delete that individual record;
  2. Or go to "Profile" / "Settings" and choose "Clear training data" to delete all local training records (irreversible).

2Delete cloud data (by email, account kept)

Cloud-synced training records can be deleted without deleting your account:

  1. Email: suyi3413067471@outlook.com
  2. Subject: Delete data
  3. In the body, provide the email address you registered with and the scope to delete (some or all cloud training data);
  4. We will complete the deletion within 7 business days and confirm by email.

To also delete the account itself, see the Account Deletion page.

3Data types deleted vs. retained

Deleted

  • Local training records: deleted individually or all at once in-app;
  • Cloud training records: deleted according to your email request scope.

Retained / not deleted

  • The account itself: deleting data does not affect your account, which remains valid (unless you separately request account deletion);
  • Logs required to be retained by law (if any).

4Retention period

  • Deletion request handling time: 7 business days;
  • Once processed, the relevant data is deleted with no additional retention (except as required by law).
