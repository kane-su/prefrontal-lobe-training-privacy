Prefrontal Trainer

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 27, 2026

Thank you for using "Prefrontal Trainer" ("the App"). We take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how the App collects, uses, and protects your information.

1What We Collect

The App is designed "local-first": the vast majority of your data stays on your device.

1.1 Local data (stored on your device by default)

  • Training & assessment data: scores, accuracy, reaction times, difficulty levels, and other records from training and baseline assessments.
  • Profile: information you voluntarily provide in the questionnaire (age band, occupation status, training experience), used to set starting difficulty and volume.
  • Settings: daily goals, sound/vibration/feedback toggles, theme preference, reminder settings, and so on.

1.2 Optional account & cloud data

Only when you actively use "Sign in / Sign up" and complete cloud-sync configuration:

  • Account information: your email address (login identifier) and a nickname you choose. Passwords are stored hashed on the server; the App never stores plaintext passwords.
  • Cloud sync: your training records are uploaded to the cloud service you configure (Supabase) only when you initiate sync, for cross-device recovery.
If you do not sign in, the App runs fully offline and uploads nothing.

1.3 What we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera data (in-app sounds are played locally; nothing is recorded).
  • We integrate no third-party advertising SDKs and perform no behavioral analytics or tracking.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third party.

2How We Use Information

  • To compute scores, adaptive difficulty, trends, and the Reliable Change Index.
  • To sync and recover your training records across devices after you sign in.
  • To enable features you configure, such as daily reminders.

We use information only to the extent necessary for these functions, and never for unrelated purposes.

3Storage & Security

  • Local data: stored in your device's secure sandbox, protected by the operating system.
  • Cloud data: transmitted using industry-standard encryption and stored with the cloud provider you configure; passwords are securely hashed.

4Your Rights

You may at any time:

  • Export: one-tap export of all your data (JSON file) in Settings.
  • Delete: delete individual training records, or permanently erase your account and local data, in Settings.
  • Opt out: sign out at any time to return to fully offline use.

5Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children. If you are the guardian of a child under 14 and believe they are using the App, please contact us.

6Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated through the App where appropriate.
